About Us

🏛️ History

St. Xavier's Institute of Technology was established in 1995 by the Xavier Educational Trust with a vision to impart quality technical education. Starting with just 3 departments and 120 students, today we have grown into a prestigious institution with 12 departments and over 5000 students.

📋 Mission

  • To provide holistic education that combines technical expertise with ethical values
  • To foster research and innovation in emerging technologies
  • To develop socially responsible professionals
  • To build strong industry-academia partnerships

🌟 Core Values

  • Excellence: Striving for the highest standards in education
  • Integrity: Upholding honesty and transparency
  • Innovation: Encouraging creative thinking and problem-solving
  • Inclusion: Fostering a diverse and inclusive learning environment
